## Core Concepts Explained

### Osoto Gari (Major Outer Reaping)
Osoto Gari is a powerful Judo throw that involves unbalancing an opponent by sweeping their leg. The technique typically begins with a strong grip on the opponent's lapel and sleeve. The thrower then steps forward with one leg, placing it behind the opponent's leg, and simultaneously pulls the opponent forward and down while reaping their leg. This action causes the opponent to lose balance and fall.

### Hip Throw (Koshi Guruma)
A hip throw, such as Koshi Guruma, utilizes the thrower's hip as a fulcrum to off-balance and throw the opponent. The thrower establishes a strong grip, often around the opponent's torso or neck. By lowering their center of gravity and rotating their hips, the thrower lifts the opponent onto their hip, using their body weight and momentum to execute the throw.

### Head Choke Escape
Escaping a head choke involves breaking the opponent's grip and creating space to breathe. This can be achieved by tucking the chin to protect the carotid arteries and using arm leverage to pry open the choke. The escape often requires a combination of movement, strength, and precise technique to disengage from the hold safely.
