# AI Knowledge Management: Wiki vs. OpenBrain

## TL;DR
AI knowledge management is evolving from simple Q&A or raw data storage to more sophisticated systems that build and maintain knowledge artifacts. This involves choosing between different memory architectures, like wikis or structured databases, each with unique strengths and weaknesses in handling information complexity, scale, and user interaction. The goal is to create AI systems that can reliably and efficiently synthesize and present information, supporting human understanding and decision-making.

## Key Takeaways
- AI knowledge management is shifting from reactive Q&A to proactive knowledge artifact maintenance.
- Wiki-based approaches excel at narrative synthesis and human readability, while database approaches offer structured query capabilities and scalability.
- The choice of AI memory architecture (e.g., wiki, vector DB, knowledge graph) impacts how AI handles information complexity, scale, and user interaction.
- Effective AI systems require intentional architecture design to balance data retrieval, synthesis, and the potential for errors.
- The future of AI in knowledge work lies in hybrid approaches that leverage the strengths of multiple memory systems.
